### convomem 6. The plateau: how many weightings are statistically tied with the best

- Best accuracy: 0.370 at (0.8, 0.1, 0.1).
- Weightings not significantly worse than the best (paired McNemar, p > 0.05): **83 of 1000** (8%).
- Published 0.6/0.3/0.1 against the best: 4 better, 14 worse, p = 0.031 -> outside the plateau.
- The 917 significantly worse weightings have mean shares semantic 0.31, lexical 0.34, recency 0.35 (the plateau: 0.56 / 0.31 / 0.13).
- With this many comparisons some 'best' is guaranteed by noise alone; see section 7 for how much.

### convomem 7. Winner's curse: does 'the best weighting' survive new questions?

Pick the best weighting on a random half of the questions, then score it on the other half. Repeated 200 times.

- Average optimism (score on the half it was picked on minus score on the unseen half): **+0.048**.
- Picked weighting against published, on the unseen half: mean +0.014; it beat published in 68% of splits, tied in 12%, lost in 20%.
