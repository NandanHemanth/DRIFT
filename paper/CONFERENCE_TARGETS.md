# Where to publish DRIFT

Archival venues only. Compiled 7 August 2026, verified against official sites on that date. Confirm each before relying on it, since dates move.

**Archival** means the work enters permanent proceedings or a journal of record with a DOI, indexed in Google Scholar and the ACL Anthology or DBLP. It counts as a publication, and it is the form that matters for a CV and for the "authorship of scholarly articles" criterion in an O-1A or EB-1A petition. It also means the paper is spent: dual-submission rules prevent publishing the same work twice.

**Constraint that shapes this list:** the author is on an F-1 visa, currently on OPT. International travel is the binding risk, so venues that require no travel, or travel only within the United States, are weighted heavily. See section 5.

## 3. The four worth acting on

### TMLR, Transactions on Machine Learning Research

<https://jmlr.org/tmlr/> · rolling submission, no deadline

**The best fit on this list, and the one most often overlooked by students.**

- **Archival and respected.** Published under the JMLR umbrella, indexed, DOI, in DBLP.
- **Rolling submission.** No deadline to miss. Reviews within roughly 4 weeks, decisions within roughly 2 months.
- **The acceptance criteria suit this paper unusually well.** TMLR asks two questions: are the claims supported by convincing evidence, and would some individuals in the community be interested in the findings. It deliberately does not gate on novelty, significance or state-of-the-art results. DRIFT is a careful controlled study with bootstrap intervals, paired significance tests, a replication across two benchmarks and an honest threats-to-validity section. That is precisely what TMLR is designed to accept and what a novelty-hungry conference reviewer tends to undervalue.
- **Journal-to-Conference track.** TMLR participates in the J2C track with NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR, so an accepted paper can be presented at one of those conferences without a second review. Roughly 10 percent of accepted papers receive J2C certification.
- **No travel required to publish.** The publication exists whether or not a conference is attended.

**Recommendation: submit here first.** It is the highest expected value, it has no deadline pressure, and a TMLR acceptance is a real publication rather than a workshop line.

### TACL, Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics

<https://transacl.org/> · deadline the 1st of every month

- **Archival journal** published by MIT Press, appears in the ACL Anthology, 2024 impact factor 15.05.
- **Monthly deadlines**, so the next one is 1 September 2026 and there is one every month after that.
- **Accepted papers earn a presentation slot at an ACL-sponsored conference of the author's choosing.** That is the key detail: publish in TACL, then present at **NAACL 2027 in San Francisco**. Journal publication plus a US conference talk, with no separate conference submission and no international travel.
- Conference-style formatting, journal-style review, so the existing paper needs little reformatting.

Harder to get into than TMLR, and review is slower. Worth attempting after a TMLR decision, or in parallel if the versions differ enough to avoid a dual-submission problem. Check TACL's concurrent submission policy before doing both.

### NAACL 2027, via ACL Rolling Review, deadline 12 October 2026

<https://aclrollingreview.org/dates> · San Francisco, 1–5 June 2027

- **San Francisco.** No international travel.
- ACL-family archival venue, which carries more weight for an agent-memory or NLP CV than a general ML venue of similar rank.
- ARR decouples review from venue. Submit to the October cycle, receive reviews, then commit to NAACL 2027. If reviews are weak, revise and commit to a later venue rather than starting over.
- 66 days out, which allows recycling and improving whatever is submitted to ICLR three weeks earlier.

### NAACL 2027 Student Research Workshop

<https://naacl2025-srw.github.io/cfp> is the 2025 call; the 2027 version will follow the same structure

- **Archival at the author's choice.** Selecting archival publishes the paper in the NAACL 2027 SRW Proceedings, in the ACL Anthology.
- **Best acceptance odds of any archival venue here**, because it is scoped to student work.
- **Includes a mentoring programme**: an experienced researcher is assigned pre-submission to advise on the paper, and again at the conference.
- San Francisco, so no international travel.
- Two categories: research papers and thesis proposals. This work fits the research paper category, and the thesis proposal category is also open.

The trade-off is prestige: an SRW paper is worth less than a NAACL main-conference paper. Treat it as the floor that guarantees an archival publication, not as the ceiling.

## 5. F-1 and OPT considerations

Not legal advice. Confirm everything with the university's designated school official before booking travel.

**Travel is the real constraint.** Re-entering the United States on OPT requires, at minimum, a valid unexpired F-1 visa stamp, a valid EAD card, an I-20 endorsed for travel within the last six months, and evidence of employment or a job offer. If the visa stamp has expired, re-entry requires a new stamp from a consulate abroad, which can trigger administrative processing under section 221(g) with no fixed timeline. A conference trip is not worth being stranded outside the country during OPT.

**This is the strongest argument for the two journals.** TMLR and TACL produce an archival publication with no travel at all, and TACL additionally grants a presentation slot at a US conference. They remove the travel question from the publication question entirely.

**Canada is a partial exception.** Automatic visa revalidation can permit re-entry from a trip of under 30 days to Canada or Mexico under specific conditions, but it does not apply to everyone, notably not to nationals of certain countries and not if a new visa application was filed while abroad. This affects the two Montréal venues. Verify individually.

**Attending or presenting is not employment.** If currently unemployed on OPT, the 90-day unemployment clock continues to run during a conference. Presenting unpaid research does not pause or extend it.

**For a future O-1A or EB-1A petition**, the relevant criteria include authorship of scholarly articles, original contributions of major significance, and judging the work of others.

- Journal articles satisfy the authorship criterion cleanly. TMLR and TACL are stronger on this axis than a conference paper of comparable selectivity, because adjudicators recognise journals more readily.
- Peer reviewing satisfies the judging criterion. Most venues require at least one prior acceptance at a major venue, so revisit reviewing invitations the moment the first paper lands.
- Open-source adoption of the DRIFT framework, measured in citations, stars and dependent projects, supports the original-contribution criterion. Keep the repository public and the release tagged.

## 6. Recommended sequence

1. **Now.** Submit to **TMLR**. Rolling, no deadline, criteria that reward exactly this kind of careful empirical work, and reviews back in about a month. This is the highest-expected-value action available.
2. **By 25 September.** Submit to **ICLR 2027** in parallel if TMLR review has not concluded, but check TMLR's concurrent submission policy first. California venue, top-tier, and public OpenReview feedback even on rejection.
3. **By 12 October.** Submit the revised paper to the **ARR October cycle** for NAACL 2027 in San Francisco. ARR reviews arrive in time to decide whether NAACL is the primary target or the fallback.
4. **~February 2027.** If nothing has landed by then, submit to the **NAACL 2027 SRW** in archival mode. Highest acceptance odds, mentoring included, still in the ACL Anthology, still San Francisco.
5. **Before any of the above.** Run the outstanding multi-agent campaign on MQuAKE-T. RQ4 is currently answered offline only, and "one of four research questions validated on synthetic data" is the easiest thing for a reviewer to reject on. The swarm harness already works; point `configs/swarm_real.json` at `data/mquake_t_sh.json`.
